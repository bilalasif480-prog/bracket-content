## 0. Reality check (read first)

- **Local pack ("web design Manchester" map results) is out of reach.** Google's map/local results need a real address or service-area business in the area. Bracket has neither and must not fake one. So: no Google Business Profile with a Manchester address, ever.
- **What can work:** (1) organic pages that answer "for Manchester businesses" honestly, (2) UK-wide service terms, (3) long-tail questions (articles), (4) directory listings that rank for these terms (Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, The Manifest, Semrush Agency Partners all have Manchester/UK category pages — seen in the search results below), and (5) GEO, where AI answers pull from those same lists and from clear pages.
- **Competitor phrasing (from web search, 22 Nov):** UK firms say "web design Manchester", "website design Greater Manchester", "app developers Manchester", "mobile app development Manchester", "AI consultants / AI automation Manchester", "local SEO Manchester", "Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)", "GEO / AEO", "AI SEO". For AI agents, UK small-business vendors (Fasthosts, BT, Bluehost UK, Phorest) mostly say **"AI receptionist"** — worth using as a secondary term for the customer agent. UK website-cost guides (startups.co.uk, Fasthosts, Hostinger UK) dominate "how much does a website cost UK", so Bracket competes there with an honest "what changes the cost" angle, not numbers.
- **No search volumes are given here** — none were measured. Bilal can check volumes free in Google Keyword Planner (set location: Manchester / United Kingdom) before launch.
- **"Agency" is in most searches** ("SEO agency Manchester"). Bracket's copy never calls itself an agency. Target "company", "services", "developers", "software house" instead; don't stuff "agency".
