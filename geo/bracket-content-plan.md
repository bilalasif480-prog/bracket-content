## 0. What people in Kuwait actually search (research, 22 Nov)

- **Cost is the #1 question, in both languages.** "How much does a website cost in Kuwait" returns a wall of cost guides from foreign/regional firms (codingclave "Website Design Cost in Kuwait 2026", appwrk "Website Development Price in Kuwait", kolonell's one-page-per-industry "website cost vs local agencies Kuwait City" series, chrisansgroup, a Blogspot "Website Pricing in Kuwait"). Arabic "كم يكلف تصميم موقع في الكويت" returns generic Arabic guides (Seahawk, GoDaddy) and Khamsat gigs — **no Kuwaiti company answers it in Arabic.**
- **Arabic intent lives on freelance boards.** "ابي اسوي موقع لمشروعي" / "تطبيق للمحل" return Mostaql/Khamsat project posts — people describe what they want and wait for bids. Those posts = the exact wording to answer.
- **WhatsApp auto-reply / chatbot:** Arabic results are all SaaS vendors (respond.io, SleekFlow, Interakt, Infobip) with Saudi/UAE focus; English Kuwait results are LetsBot's industry × Kuwait pages and WATI "GEO" pages for clinics. Nothing from a Kuwait builder explaining "bot vs agent vs WhatsApp Business app".
- **"Show up in ChatGPT":** English is saturated (HubSpot, Neil Patel, seo.com). Arabic has 1–2 translated vendor posts. Kuwait-specific: none → easy GEO win in Arabic.
- **Kuwait-only facts people need:** KNET payment gateway (bank/gateway agreements, Tap, UPayments) and Arabic RTL — a real local angle foreign cost guides skip.
- **"Do I need a website if I sell on Instagram?"** — answered only by global hosts (GoDaddy, Shopify, Hostinger). Kuwait businesses run on Instagram + WhatsApp → strong fit.

Takeaway: win on **Arabic** + **Kuwait-specific practical detail** + **honest "what changes the cost" (no numbers)**. Every article ends in "Tell us what you need → Get a quote" (contact.html + WhatsApp).

### #1 — What changes the cost of a website in Kuwait
- **EN:** What decides the cost of a website in Kuwait? (A plain guide before you ask for quotes)
- **AR:** كم يكلف تصميم موقع في الكويت؟ شنو اللي يحدد السعر قبل لا تطلب عرض
- **Slug:** `articles/website-cost-kuwait.html` · `ar/articles/website-cost-kuwait.html`
- **Target:** EN "how much does a website cost in Kuwait", "website design price Kuwait" · AR "كم يكلف تصميم موقع في الكويت", "سعر تصميم موقع الكويت"
- **Intent:** commercial research, close to buying. **Links to:** `websites.html` (+ `seo.html`)
- **Why first:** biggest demand in both languages; Arabic side has no Kuwaiti answer.
- **Answer-first:** "There is no honest single price. The cost of a website in Kuwait depends on how many pages and languages it has, whether it sells online (KNET/card payments), who writes the content, what it must connect to, and who looks after it after launch. Here is what moves the cost, so you can compare quotes fairly."
- **Outline (H2s):**
  1. Why two quotes for "a website" can be so different — they are often quoting different things.
  2. The 8 things that change the cost — type (landing page / business site / online store / web app), number of pages, Arabic + English (RTL done properly is real work, not a toggle), design (template vs custom), content (who writes text, photos), features (booking, forms, WhatsApp, accounts), payments (KNET/card gateway = gateway setup + testing), integrations (CRM, delivery, POS), after launch (hosting, domain, updates, support).
  3. Costs that are not the build — domain, hosting, payment gateway fees, email, paid plugins/themes; who should own the domain and accounts (you).
  4. Template builder vs custom-built — when each makes sense (honest: a template can be right for some).
  5. How to get a quote you can compare — what to send: what the site is for, pages, languages, features, examples you like, deadline. Ask every quote for: what's included, what's not, who owns what.
  6. Red flags in a website quote — no written scope, you don't own the domain, no Arabic check, no "checked on phones before launch", no plan for after launch.
  7. How Bracket quotes — written scope with what's included and not, then a quote; no prices on the site because every site differs. CTA.
- **FAQ:** Can I get a price without a call? (send the brief on WhatsApp/form, we reply with a scope and quote) · Is a bilingual site more expensive than English only? (it's more work: two sets of text, RTL layout, checking both — say so, no number) · Do I pay yearly after launch? (hosting/domain renew; explain what renews) · Can I start small and add later? (yes, landing page first, store later).
- **No numbers.** If Bilal ever wants a range, only quote a cited public source and label it clearly as not Bracket's price.
