## What would flip a no

- A local owner I trust telling me he built their site and that he was reliable afterwards.
- Seeing proof it brings in more bookings than my Fresha page does, like a local Manchester business similar to mine showing the numbers, and a care plan I can cancel any month or skip completely.
- Proof that a proper site gets a business like mine more bookings than Fresha and Instagram do, like a UK salon or barber with real numbers.
- A handful of real, named UK reviews on Google or Trustpilot from businesses like mine, ideally ones I could call or visit.
- A couple of real Manchester businesses like mine I could ring and ask about him, so he can sit in my three quotes as a proper option.
- Two or three real UK small business owners, ideally in Greater Manchester, I could message on WhatsApp who'd tell me he delivered and still answers after launch.
- Someone in my local Facebook group posting that he did their site and it actually brought in more bookings than their Booksy page.
- A 10-minute video call where he shows me a site he's already mocked up for my business, plus one UK owner I can actually message who says he delivered.
- Another Manchester owner I trust telling me he built their site, it was painless and he still answers their WhatsApps months later.
- A written delivery date with a penalty if he misses it, plus one real UK owner I can message who says he finished on time.
- Talking on the phone to a real UK business owner he's already built for, who tells me he finished on time and still answers when something breaks.
- A couple of real Manchester businesses I can ring who say he's sorted everything for them, plus a clear promise in writing of what the £43 covers and that I can leave with my site anytime.
