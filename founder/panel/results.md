## What would flip a no

- A local owner I trust telling me he built their site and it went well.
- Hard numbers showing a local business like mine got noticeably more bookings from a proper site than from Fresha plus Instagram, from a UK owner I could actually message.
- Seeing a real UK salon or barber like mine, with numbers showing they got more bookings or new customers from Google that Fresha and Instagram weren't bringing in.
- A handful of real Google or Trustpilot reviews from UK small businesses, ideally Manchester, plus a process where I fill in a short form instead of doing calls.
- A few reviews from Manchester businesses my size that I could ring, plus a fixed quote I can put next to two local ones without having to get on a call.
- Two or three real UK clients, ideally in Manchester, that I could message or look at their live sites, plus a quote that sits closer to what local firms charge so I'm comparing like for like.
- A couple of real Manchester businesses, ideally salons, I can look up and message, or someone in my local Facebook group vouching for him.
- Seeing a real UK business he's done a site for that I could actually ring or visit, plus a quick video call where he shows me exactly what he'd do and that I won't have to lift a finger.
- Another Manchester owner I know telling me he built their site, it was painless and he's still answering their WhatsApps months later.
- Speaking to a real client, even the Kuwait AC company, on a quick call or WhatsApp, plus a live site of theirs I can click through, and a fixed delivery date in writing.
- Speaking to a real UK business owner he's already built for and looked after for a few months, plus a written promise of how fast he answers when the site breaks.
- A couple of real UK small business owners, ideally in Manchester, I could ring or read reviews from saying he's still looking after their site months later, plus hosting folded into one simple yearly price.
