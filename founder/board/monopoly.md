# Monopoly lens
Score: 3/10 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- An AI-agent build team could be a real cost advantage: one person shipping design, build, QA and security testing. That is the start of a scale economy, if it holds up.
- The founding offer (built first, pay only when happy) takes away the buyer's risk, which matters when there is no proof yet.
- The limits are honest: no promised rankings, no invented reviews, no pretend UK office. A brand built on that is slow to grow but hard to fake.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No small market to own.** The site says "UK and internationally", the profile says Manchester first, and the buyer is any small business wanting a website. That is one of the most crowded markets there is: local freelancers, agencies, overseas builders and DIY site builders all sell to the same buyer. Nothing here starts with a big share of anything.
2. **No secret and no 10x.** "Ready for Google", "we write the text" and "pay when happy" are improvements, not a breakthrough. A rival can copy every one of them by month six, and the AI-agent advantage sits behind the scenes, so the buyer never sees it. If anything better shows up, it shows up as a lower price, and price is where competition eats the profit.
3. **No distribution.** There is no stated route to the first 100 customers, no marketing budget, no weekly hours and no known cost to win a client, and the business is run part time from Kuwait with a +965 number and a Gmail address, selling to UK buyers who meet it cold. A good product with no way to reach buyers is not a business.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What is the one narrow group (one trade, one town, one platform) where Bracket could be the obvious choice to more than half the buyers within a year?
- What do you know about that group that the agencies selling to them don't?
- How exactly do the first 10 clients arrive, and what will each one cost in time and money?
- What does the AI-agent team let you do that a buyer would notice: 10x faster, 10x cheaper, or something no one else offers? Which one?
- How many hours a week can you give this next to your full-time job?
- In ten years, what stops a cheaper builder using the same AI tools from taking your place?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
Not applicable, the vote is PASS. What would move it to FUND IF:
- Pick one small market by name (one trade in one UK city, for example) and drop "internationally".
- Name a buyer-visible advantage roughly 10x better than the alternative on something that group cares about, not just a lower price.
- Show a repeatable way to reach buyers, with a measured cost per client from the first founding clients.
- Turn the founding 5 into public proof inside that same narrow market, so each client makes the next one easier to win.
