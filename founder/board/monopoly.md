# Monopoly lens
Score: 3/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- Low cost base: one person plus AI agents, so it can survive on a few clients a year while it looks for its niche.
- Honest trust lines (written scope, client owns code, "no UK office" stated, no promised rankings) are better than many agency sites.
- GEO and AI agents are newer categories than websites. If any part of this can become a small market Bracket leads, it is there.
- The "build with AI agents, QA-checked" process could become real know-how, but only if it delivers something clearly better, not just cheaper.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No monopoly, no small market.** Five equal services for "UK SMEs", then "UK and international", is the opposite of starting with a big share of a small market. Websites, apps and SEO for small businesses are crowded. How many UK agencies, freelancers and DIY builders already serve the same buyer is unknown, but the lens assumes many until shown otherwise. The site has already dropped the one narrow start it had (the Manchester page).
2. **No distribution.** No clients, no case studies, no stated budget, hours or cost to win a client, and the founder is remote from Kuwait with a +965 number and no UK office. There is no plan for how the first 100 customers arrive, and right now not even the first one.
3. **No secret, no durability.** The AI-agent build method is open to anyone with the same tools. Nothing stops a copycat in month six, and the "AI builds it" edge may shrink as buyers learn to use the same tools themselves. No network effects, scale or brand yet.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What is the one small market you could own: one service, one trade, one place? Who leads it today?
- What do you know about that buyer that UK competitors miss?
- On the one thing that matters to that buyer, where are you about ten times better, not 20% cheaper?
- How do the first 10 clients arrive, through which channel, and what does each cost to win? (Unknown today.)
- How many hours a week do you have alongside your full-time job?
- Why now: what changed in AI search or AI agents that makes this the year to start?
- What will still protect you in ten years once the AI tools are everywhere?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Pick one wedge and make it the whole front page. For example, one service (a WhatsApp booking agent, or GEO) for one trade in one city. Shelve the other services until that one is won.
2. Name the secret in one sentence and show proof of it: a working demo for that trade, and one real client result.
3. Write a distribution plan for the first 10 clients with the channel, the weekly hours, and a measured cost per client after the first test.
4. Set a review point: if the wedge has no paying UK client by an agreed date, change the wedge rather than add services.
