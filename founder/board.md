## Risks all three raised
1. **Unfinished shop window.** "TODO for Bilal" placeholders show on the live case study page; no domain, no deployment, Gmail, +965 WhatsApp. "A buyer who sees an unfinished page will expect an unfinished site."
2. **Outreach and site don't match.** DMs will go to UK cafés, but the site says "UK and internationally" and never mentions cafés. The case study is a Kuwait AC firm, which says little to a Manchester café.
3. **Generic promise, crowded market.** "Websites for small businesses, ready for Google" is what every rival says; pay-when-happy is copyable.

Raised by two: too many services for one part-time person; no hours, reply time or cost per client.
