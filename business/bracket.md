## 2. What it sells
- **In one line:** Bracket builds AI agents that take a real job off a company's team.
- **Services:**
  - **Customer agent** — answers customers and books appointments on WhatsApp and website chat.
  - **Sales agent** — finds companies that fit the client's ideal customer and drafts the first message.
  - **Marketing agent** — studies the niche, writes hooks, captions and scripts, prepares posts.
  - **Custom agent** — any repeat task that follows rules (reports, reminders, data entry, document checks).
- **Does NOT do (since 10 Nov 2026):** website building, app building, QA testing as services.
  Bracket may still build a page or test something *as part of an agent project*, but it is
  never sold or advertised on its own. Never mention QA, "we break it", website design or
  app development as a Bracket service.

## 3. Who it sells to
- **Customer:** small and medium businesses in Kuwait and the Gulf — clinics, salons, gyms,
  repair and home services, real estate agencies, contractors, shops and online stores.
- **Where they are:** Instagram and WhatsApp.
- **What they worry about:** slow replies losing customers, leads going cold, social media
  going quiet, and staff time eaten by repeat work.

## 4. Voice
- **Languages:** English + natural Gulf Arabic. On screen: English headline with Arabic under it
  at ~70% size. In captions: Arabic first, then a `—` line, then English. Mixed hashtags.
- **Tone:** short, plain, direct. No hype, no corporate filler.
- **Slogan:** "AI that gets it done."
- **Words to use:** agent, takes the job off your team, answers, books, hands over to a person,
  you approve, checked before launch.
- **Words to avoid:** fixed price, fixed scope, ship date, hourly, QA, testing as a service,
  website design, app development, guaranteed results.
