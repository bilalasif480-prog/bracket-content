## What would flip a no

- A local owner I trust telling me they built their site and it's been solid for a while.
- Proof it actually gets more bookings than my Fresha page does, like a UK business my size showing real numbers. Plus one fixed price in writing that covers everything, with no monthly fees after launch.
- Proof it gets more bookings than my Booksy page alone, like a real UK salon or barber they've done showing the numbers, and someone I can actually get on WhatsApp after hours.
- Seeing a few genuine Google or Trustpilot reviews from other Manchester small businesses, plus live sites they've actually built that I can click through.
- Seeing two or three live sites they've built for Manchester businesses like mine, with owners I could ring, plus a written support promise with UK-hours response times.
- A couple of real Manchester businesses I can look up and message about their site, plus a written contract under UK law that says the domain is registered in my name from day one.
- Someone in my local business group I trust saying they used them and it actually brought them more bookings, not just a nicer-looking page.
- A 10-minute video call where I see a real site they've built and a fixed written price that covers a couple of rounds of changes, with the domain registered in my name from day one.
- Another Manchester owner I know telling me they built their site and it was painless, done mostly over WhatsApp without a load of calls.
- Talking to one or two real UK business owners they've already built for, plus a written monthly support plan with a guaranteed response time when something goes wrong.
- One fixed, all-in price that includes hosting, domain and small updates for a low set monthly fee, written down, so I never have to touch the tech.
- Two or three real UK small businesses like mine that they've built for, which I could actually look at and ring, plus a clear fixed yearly price for hosting and looking after it so I know there's no surprise monthly bill.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
