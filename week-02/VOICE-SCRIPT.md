# Bracket reels: your voice-over script

## How to record (5 min per reel, phone is fine)

1. Quiet room, phone close (a hand-width from your mouth), Voice Memos / Recorder app.
2. Talk like you're telling a friend, not reading. Smile a little. Short and punchy.
3. ONE take per reel. Start recording, wait 1 second, then read the lines in order.
4. Leave a clear pause of about 1 second between lines. That is how I split them and snap each one to the right moment.
5. Don't talk after the last line (the end card plays after).
6. Send me each recording here, named with the reel number (e.g. reel25.m4a). I mix it with the video and send back the finished reel.
7. English lines below. If you prefer Gulf Arabic, say the Arabic text that's on screen instead; tell me which.

Times = when the line should START. 'Finish by' = the voice must be done before then.
