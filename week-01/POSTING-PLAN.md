# Bracket Instagram: Week 1 (1–9 Nov 2026)

Post every day at **8:00 PM Kuwait time**.

How to schedule all of this in one go: go to business.facebook.com, open **Planner** and choose **Create post** or **Create reel**. Add the file and caption, then click **Schedule** and pick the date below.
Reels: add a trending sound in the Instagram app or Business Suite (the files are silent).
Carousels: upload all the slides for that day, in order (slide1, slide2, …).

> Captions use "Link in bio" as the call to action (the "DM us SITE" offer was dropped). Make sure the Instagram bio has a link to the site.
