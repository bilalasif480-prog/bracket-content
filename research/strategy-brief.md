# Strategy brief (Instapulse) · updated Fri 6 Nov 2026

For instamake (week 02 pack) and the research agent. **Early-stage mode** (Metricool: 1 follower, 5 posts), so this leans more on `niche-report.md` than on Bracket's own numbers. Full numbers: `analytics/week-01.md`.

## Growth goal (Bilal, 6 Nov): 3,000 real followers by ~6 Dec 2026
Starting point: Metricool shows 1 follower. This is a stretch goal; organic posting alone is unlikely to get there in 30 days, so every lever below runs at once. Real followers only: never buy followers, likes or views, and never use follow/unfollow or engagement bots (fake accounts hurt reach and can get the account restricted).
- **Volume (instamake):** 1 normal REEL a day at 8 PM + **trial reels** (Metricool `instagramData.type: "TRIAL_REEL"`; shown to non-followers first, so they test ideas without cluttering the grid). Ramp: **week 2 = 4 trial reels a day**, spread 10 AM–11 PM Kuwait, at least 2 h apart. If they hold up (no drop in views per reel, each one clearly different), Instapulse raises it to 6, then up to 10 a day. Never post near-duplicates: Instagram can treat repeated, low-effort uploads as spam and cut reach. Cut carousels to 1 a week (both got 0–1 reach). Reels 9–15 s, a question hook in the first second, loop the ending into the start.
- **Trial-reel testing loop:** each idea goes out as 3–4 trial reels that change ONE thing (hook line, first frame, business type: clinic / restaurant / store, or length). Name them in SCHEDULED.md as `idea-X / variant-A…D` with what changed. Every Friday, Instapulse ranks them by views, 3-second hold and average watch time; the winning hook/angle becomes next week's normal 8 PM reel and goes into the Hooks bank, and the losers are dropped. Render cost is small (~30 s per reel), so variants come from the same animation with a different opening line, voice line or end frame.
- **Repeat winners:** any reel or trial reel that beats the account's median views by 2× gets 2 new variations the next week (same idea, new hook or new business type). Instapulse flags them on Fridays.
- **Shareable topics:** "Is your site losing customers?" style self-checks for Kuwaiti shop, clinic and restaurant owners: things people send to a friend or partner. CTA in the caption: "Send this to someone with a website" / "أرسلها لصاحب موقع".
- **Collabs (Bilal):** Instagram Collab posts (Metricool `collaborators`) with real Kuwaiti small businesses, e.g. a free 2-minute website/app check reel posted together, only with their written OK. Their followers see it. Aim for 1–2 a week.
- **Daily engagement (Bilal, 20–30 min, can't be automated):** reply to every comment within an hour; leave real comments on 10–15 posts from Kuwaiti business, startup and dev accounts; answer DMs the same day.
- **Paid boost (Bilal's decision and budget):** promoting the best reel each week to Kuwait is the most dependable way to add followers fast. Instapulse will say which reel to boost; Bilal sets the budget in the Instagram app or Meta. No spend happens without him.
- **Profile:** clear bio (what Bracket does + "Kuwait" + link), and highlights Build / Test / Process, so new visitors follow.
- **Tracking:** Instapulse reports every Friday: followers, gained per week, follows per reel, and whether the pace is on track. Weekly goals to check: ~300 by 13 Nov, ~900 by 20 Nov, ~1,800 by 27 Nov, 3,000 by 6 Dec. These are targets to aim at, not forecasts.

## What's working / what to stop
- **Reels carry the account.** Reels reached 18–74 accounts each; both carousels reached 0–1. With almost no followers, carousels get no distribution. → Next week mostly reels; keep at most 1 carousel and post it at 8 PM, not late.
- **Short, blunt opening line holds people.** "No hourly clock." had 37.9% of viewers past 3 s vs 14.8–22% for the others. → Open with 2–4 big words on screen in the first second, then the problem.
- **Watch time is low** (4–5 s on 18–22 s reels; Metricool's 2026 average is 8.5 s). → Try 12–15 s cuts, put the payoff earlier, keep the loop tight.
- **Voice-over on every reel from week 2 (Bilal approved 6 Nov): use `audio-kit/`** (see its README). English-only voice: **default `["am_fenrir", 0.95, -1.5]`** (deep, calm American male; Bilal picked it 6 Nov, use it on every reel), question hook in the first second, short lines (about 4, calm pace; voice must end before the end card), original upbeat music + whoosh/tap/ding/impact SFX. Never say "Bracket" in the voice-over; stop speaking before the end card. Never rip audio or voices from other accounts. On-screen text and captions stay bilingual. Phone-safe mp4 settings are in the README. Also keep a no-audio version of each reel, so a trending sound can be added in the app. Week 1 reels stay as scheduled.
- **Stop:** posting carousels off-slot (Day 2 at 22:30), long intros before the first big text.
- Topic build vs test: no clear winner yet; keep Bilal's ask of at least one BUILD reel.

## Audio for instamake (Bilal, 6 Nov: find trending sounds and add them when they fit)
Each reel ships two ways: (a) `audio-kit` voice + our original music, or (b) `audio-kit` voice + SFX **without music** (`audio-<voice>-nomusic.wav`) with an Instagram trending sound attached in Metricool. Use (b) when a trending sound below fits the reel's mood; otherwise (a).
- Attach via Metricool `instagramData.audioConfiguration`: `audioId` = "<title> <artist>" (resolves to Instagram's licensed catalog), `audioVolume` 15–20, `videoVolume` 100 so the voice stays on top. If it is rejected (not in the business library, or ambiguous), retry once with the exact id from the error's candidate list; if still no, post version (a) and note it in SCHEDULED.md. Never download or rip any song or anyone's voice.
