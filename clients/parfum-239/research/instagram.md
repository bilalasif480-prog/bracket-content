## Risks to raise with Bilal
- Their ads put **Dior/Chanel trademarks and bottle photos** side by side with their own and claim "same scent 100%".
  That is a trademark/comparative-advertising risk, and Instagram can take down reels for it. For anything Bracket
  makes: say "inspired by" in small text, never show the designer bottle or logo as the hero, and never claim "identical".
- A comment asking "where is your shop" means the ordering path isn't clear: every reel should end with "DM to order".
