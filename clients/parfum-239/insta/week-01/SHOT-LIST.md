# 239 Parfum: shot list for the owner (month 1)

For Bilal to send to the owner. Film on a phone, **vertical (9:16), 4K or 1080p, 30 fps**, 3–8 s
per clip, no talking needed, no music. Hold the phone steady (lean on a table) or move very slowly.
Light: a window in daytime (side light, no direct sun) or one warm lamp at night; turn off
overhead lights. Dark background (black cloth, dark wood) to match the brand. No designer bottles,
logos or names in shot. Faces only with that person's consent. Send the original files (AirDrop /
Google Drive), not WhatsApp-compressed.
