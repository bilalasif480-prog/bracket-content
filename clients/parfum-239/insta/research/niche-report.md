## Format cards

### Card 1: Creator "my top 3" with price reveal (@detailsper)
- **URL:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DerXVpviNiX/ · 273K views vs 5.5K median (50×) · collab post with a Kuwaiti creator
- **Hook mechanism:** list ("my 3 favourite perfumes") + price shock
- **First frame:** man in white dishdasha on a plain beige wall, OK-hand gesture; big bold text in two pills: black pill "أحلى 3 عطور" + red pill "عندي 😍"
- **Structure:** 0–3 s list promise → 3–12 s holds up a designer bottle, black pill with its name + red pill with its high price → ~12–18 s cut to the shop interior (gold-lit shelves) with "this is where you get them for [low price]" + shocked emoji → 18–22 s staff member at the branded counter, pills listing branch locations
- **Length / pacing:** 22 s; ~2 cuts per 5 s
- **Text style:** two stacked rounded pills (black/white + red/white), bold Arabic, centred top third; emoji at line end
- **Audio:** voice (creator speaking Gulf Arabic)
- **CTA:** branch locations on screen; tag of the shop in caption
- **Why it likely worked:** list promise holds attention to the end; a concrete big price vs a small price is an instant "wait, what?" moment; a known local face; collab post doubles distribution.

### Card 2: Owner talking head, "focus with me" + offer (@zara.atamer.kw)
- **URL:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfDJ2kRNFJK/ · 54.9K vs 528 median (104×)
- **Hook mechanism:** direct address / pattern interrupt ("concentrate with me"), then offer
- **First frame:** owner seated at a wooden table in the shop, finger pointing at camera, a bottle in the foreground; gold rounded subtitle pill "ركّزو معي"
- **Structure:** 0–3 s "focus with me" → 3–40 s one-take sales talk, picks up and sprays the bottle, jokes ("by your eyes, no…") → ~40–72 s brand logo card appears top, he laughs, ends on price/discount
- **Length / pacing:** 73 s; almost no cuts (one take), word-by-word subtitles keep it moving
- **Text style:** gold pill with dark Arabic text, lower third, 2–4 words at a time
- **Audio:** voice only (Levantine/Gulf mix)
- **CTA:** website link in caption; limited-time offer
- **Why it likely worked:** a real person with humour and confidence; a big % discount and a low per-bottle price; long watch time from a single conversational take.

### Card 3: Offer reveal on a dark wall (@atyabalsheikh)
- **URL:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/De6eje_MOrP/ · 75.9K vs 12.6K median (6×) · collab with a Kuwaiti creator; ~4.5K shares
- **Hook mechanism:** offer / "chance you can't miss" reveal
- **First frame:** embossed metallic logo on a dark grey wall with palm-leaf shadows; white text "خصم 20%" above it
- **Structure:** 0–4 s logo + discount → 4–14 s slow push-in on one bottle on glass, text "فرصة ما تتعوض" → 14–24 s bottles in warm-lit wall niches, slow pan → 24–27 s end
- **Length / pacing:** 27 s; ~1 cut per 5 s, slow camera moves
- **Text style:** thin white Arabic, top third, one short line per scene
- **Audio:** music (no voice heard in sampled frames)
- **CTA:** "visit the exhibition and ask for [new scent]" (caption)
- **Why it likely worked:** looks premium yet says "deal" in the first second; shareable ("tag who should go"); collab reach. Fully reproducible without a person on camera.

### Card 4: Street blind sniff / guess (@jeremyfragrance)
- **URL:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfw0mSfs1To/ · 5.6M vs 101K median (56×) · ~50K shares
- **Hook mechanism:** blind test / guess-the-scent challenge
- **First frame:** shaky handheld two-shot on a street, a stranger sniffing a wrist, letterboxed 16:9 inside the vertical frame
- **Structure:** 0–3 s sniff → 3–8 s the stranger names a famous scent, caption shows the guess → 8–16 s cut to the creator in his studio reacting

- **Audio:** voice + reaction
- **CTA:** none on screen
- **Why it likely worked:** "can they guess it?" makes people watch to the answer and send it to friends; short; a reaction payoff.
