## 1. Who to find
- Independent cafés in **Manchester**: coffee shops, brunch spots, bakeries with seating, tea rooms.
- **Group A:** no website (only Instagram or a Google Maps listing).
- **Group B:** a weak website.
- **Skip:** chains (Costa, Starbucks, Caffè Nero, Pret, etc.) and cafés that already have a good site with booking or ordering.
- **Sources:** Google Maps, Instagram, public directories. Public business info only.

## 2. Check each café's problem
- Look at their website, Instagram and Google Maps listing; note their specific problems, e.g. no website, no table booking, no online/click-and-collect ordering, menu only as a PDF or photo, no opening hours, broken or slow on phones, outdated design.
- Check Companies House: **limited company** or **sole trader** (decides whether email is allowed, see 6).

## 3. The offer (limited-time, outreach only)
- A special, **limited-time offer only for a few Manchester cafés we contact directly**. It is NOT on the website; the website stays as it is.
- The message says clearly it's a limited-time offer for a few Manchester cafés, so it doesn't clash with the site's normal pricing.
- Sell the **solution to their problem**, not "a website".
- The website and booking system are **free of cost**.
- The café pays for its **own domain and hosting directly, in its own name**. No hosting price in the first message. If asked: "usually a few pounds a month, and I'll help you set it up."
- In return, if they're happy: an **honest public review** and permission for Bracket to show their site as a case study.

## 4. The message
- A short, personal **Instagram DM** per café, written around its specific problem and the result (e.g. "let customers book a table without calling you", "take click-and-collect orders while you're busy").
- Signed as **Bilal from Bracket**. Honest: one person, working remotely. Never suggest a UK office or a team.
- **No links in the first message.** Link to the café page (`cafes.html`) only after they reply.
- British English, short, friendly, no hype. Never invent clients, results or reviews.

## 5. Demo flow
- For the best leads, hand them to the **PM**, who has `websitebuilder` make a quick demo for that café, checked by `websiteqa` (and `bracket-hacker` per the PM's build rule), on a **private preview link clearly labelled as a free concept**. The salesman then drafts a message with the demo.

## 6. Channels and rules
- **Instagram DM first.** Email only to **limited companies**, with an easy opt-out. **No cold WhatsApp messages and no cold texts.**
- **Pace:** 10 messages per batch, then wait 6 hours. **Max 2 batches (20 messages) a day.**
- **Send times (Bilal, 25 Nov 2026): weekdays only (Mon–Fri), never weekends.**
  - Batch 1: about **9–10 a.m. UK time** (12–1 p.m. Kuwait in winter).
  - Batch 2: about **3–4 p.m. UK time** (6–7 p.m. Kuwait in winter).
  - Never send during the **12–2 p.m. UK lunch rush**.
  - Kuwait is 3 hours ahead of the UK in winter, 2 hours in British Summer Time (from late March): then the Kuwait times are 11 a.m.–12 p.m. and 5–6 p.m. Always go by UK time.
- **Stop** if Instagram shows any warning or block.
- **Every batch needs Bilal's yes in chat** before anything is sent. Never reply to anyone without his yes.

### 8b. Score every lead (0–100) and grade it
| Points | Signal |
|---|---|
| 25 | Problem size: no website (25) / weak site with no booking or ordering (15–20) / small issues (5) |
| 20 | Active: posted on Instagram in the last 30 days, replies to comments |
| 15 | Busy: lots of Google reviews or Instagram engagement (they'd lose real custom) |
| 15 | Fit: table service or takeaway, so booking/ordering clearly helps |
| 15 | Reachable: owner-run account, DMs open |
| 10 | Independent, one or two sites (not a chain or franchise) |
Grade: **A 80+** (send first; candidate for a demo) · **B 60–79** (send) · **C under 60** (skip for now). Log the score and grade. Each batch of 10 is filled from A, then B.

### 8c. Follow-ups
- If no reply after **3 weekdays**, draft **one** short, friendly follow-up (new angle, no pressure, still no link). If no reply after that, mark **no** and stop. Never more than 2 messages in total to a café that hasn't replied.
- Follow-ups count towards the 20-a-day limit and need Bilal's yes like any batch.

### 8d. Objection answers (ready replies for Bilal to approve)
Keep these as drafts the salesman adapts per café:
- "What's the catch?" → It's free for a few cafés because I need real UK examples; you'd only cover your own domain and hosting, in your name, and an honest review if you're happy.
- "How much is hosting?" → Usually a few pounds a month; I'll help you set it up in your name.
- "You're not in the UK." → True, I work remotely from Kuwait at UK-friendly hours, by WhatsApp, email and video call; your domain and site stay yours whatever happens.
- "I already have Fresha / Instagram." → Keep them; the site adds your menu, hours and booking in one place Google can find, and links to them.
- "Who fixes it if it breaks?" → I do; the care plan is optional and can be cancelled any month, and you can move the site any time.
- "No time." → One short call, or a form; I write the text and do the rest.
Never promise results, rankings or reply times.
