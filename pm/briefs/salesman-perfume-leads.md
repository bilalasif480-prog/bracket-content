## Who to find (for now, only these)

Perfume brands that sell on Instagram (Kuwait / Gulf first) and have **no website**.

### Group A: new brands
- New perfume business on Instagram (recent account, few posts, still growing).
- No website (no link in bio, or bio link is only WhatsApp / Linktree to WhatsApp).
- Pitch angle: start properly with a simple online store from day one.

### Group B: established brands
- Older perfume business with a lot of followers.
- Takes orders **only on WhatsApp / DMs**, no website.
- Pitch angle: they're losing time and sales handling every order by hand; an online store (and app later) takes orders 24/7.

## Skip
- Anyone who already has a working website or online store (Shopify, Salla, Zid, etc.).
- Big international brands and resellers of them with official sites.
- Existing Bracket clients and live pitches (239 Parfum, Scentora).

## For each lead, record
Handle, group (A or B), follower count (as shown, not guessed), how they take orders,
WhatsApp listed? (yes/no, never the number), why they fit, and message drafts (EN + Gulf Arabic):
- Instagram DM draft (for every lead)
- WhatsApp draft (only if they list WhatsApp for orders), slightly different wording from the DM
